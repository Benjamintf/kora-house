The Kora story

A softer way to discover Addis.

Named for the Ethiopian kora, our house is shaped around rhythm, conversation, craft, and the generous spirit of home. We pair locally inspired interiors with thoughtful service and a calm garden setting.

From a morning buna ceremony to a late-night tasting menu, every stay is designed to feel personal rather than prescribed.
